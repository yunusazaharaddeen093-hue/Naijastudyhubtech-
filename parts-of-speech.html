Parts of Speech Explained

Master the Eight Parts of Speech with Examples and Exercises

What are Parts of Speech?

Parts of speech are the different types of words used in a sentence. Each word serves a specific purpose or function. Understanding parts of speech helps us write and speak correctly.

There are 8 main parts of speech: Nouns, Verbs, Adjectives, Adverbs, Pronouns, Prepositions, Conjunctions, and Interjections.

1. NOUNS

Definition:

A word that names a person, place, thing, or idea.

Examples: girl, Lagos, book, love, happiness, teacher, river, elephant

In a sentence: Chioma is a student.

Types of Nouns:

  • Proper Nouns: Names specific people or places (Always capitalized) - Nigeria, Lagos, Chike, Monday
  • Common Nouns: General names - girl, country, day
  • Collective Nouns: Names a group - family, team, class
  • Abstract Nouns: Names ideas or feelings - love, courage, freedom

2. VERBS

Definition:

A word that shows action or a state of being.

Examples: run, walk, eat, sleep, is, are, become, seem

In a sentence: She runs to school.

Action Verbs: jump, dance, write, read
Linking Verbs: is, are, am, was, were, be, become, seem

3. ADJECTIVES

Definition:

A word that describes or modifies a noun. It tells us more about the noun.

Examples: big, small, beautiful, red, happy, cold, lazy

In a sentence: She wore a blue dress.

Adjectives answering:
• What kind? → beautiful, big, small, sad
• Which one? → this, that, these, those
• How many? → many, few, some, several

4. ADVERBS

Definition:

A word that describes or modifies a verb, adjective, or another adverb. It tells us how, when, where, or why something happens.

Examples: quickly, slowly, today, yesterday, here, there, very, really

In a sentence: She ran quickly down the street.

Adverbs answering:
• How? → quickly, slowly, carefully, happily
• When? → today, yesterday, tomorrow, now
• Where? → here, there, outside, inside
• To what extent? → very, really, quite, too

5. PRONOUNS

Definition:

A word that takes the place of a noun to avoid repetition.

Examples: I, you, he, she, it, we, they, me, him, her, us, them

In a sentence: Kofi is smart. He is smart. (He replaces Kofi)

Personal Pronouns:
• Subject: I, you, he, she, it, we, they
• Object: me, you, him, her, it, us, them

6. PREPOSITIONS

Definition:

A word that shows the relationship between a noun and other words in the sentence. It shows position or direction.

Examples: in, on, at, by, to, from, under, over, between, during

In a sentence: The book is on the table.

Common Prepositions:
in, on, at, by, to, from, under, over, between, through, during, before, after, above, below, beside, near, behind

7. CONJUNCTIONS

Definition:

A word that joins words, phrases, or clauses together.

Examples: and, but, or, because, if, while, although

In a sentence: I like football and basketball.

Common Conjunctions:
• And (adds ideas)
• But (shows contrast)
• Or (shows choice)
• Because (shows reason)
• If (shows condition)
• Although (shows contrast)

8. INTERJECTIONS

Definition:

A word or phrase that expresses strong emotion or surprise. It stands alone or is loosely connected to the sentence.

Examples: Oh!, Wow!, Hey!, Ouch!, Yay!, Alas!, Help!

In a sentence: Oh! I didn't see you there.

Identifying Parts of Speech - Practice Sentence

Sentence: "Yesterday, the beautiful girl walked quickly to the market."

• Yesterday = Adverb
• the = Article
• beautiful = Adjective
• girl = Noun
• walked = Verb
• quickly = Adverb
• to = Preposition
• the = Article
• market = Noun
Quick Tips:
  • Nouns name things
  • Verbs show action or state of being
  • Adjectives describe nouns
  • Adverbs describe verbs or adjectives
  • Pronouns replace nouns
  • Prepositions show relationships and position
  • Conjunctions join words or clauses
  • Interjections express emotion

Practice Questions

  1. Identify the noun in: "The cat sleeps on the bed."
  2. What is the verb in: "She sings beautifully"?
  3. Find the adjective: "I like cold water."
  4. Which word is an adverb? "The quick fox jumps high over the wall."
  5. Replace the noun with a pronoun: "Ade is tall. _____ is tall."
  6. Identify the preposition: "The pen is under the chair."
  7. What conjunction is used? "I wanted to go, but it rained."
  8. Is "Ouch!" an interjection? (Yes/No)
