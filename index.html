    Senior Secondary specialisation

    Science & Arts subjects

    From SSS 1, students choose a track. Here's a quick look at what each core subject covers.

    Science Subjects

    • Physics — the study of matter, energy, motion, and the laws that govern the physical world.
    • Chemistry — elements, compounds, and reactions: understanding what things are made of and how they change.
    • Biology — living organisms, from cells to ecosystems, and how life functions and survives.
    • Further Mathematics — advanced mathematical concepts for students headed into engineering, technology, or the sciences.
    • Agricultural Science — crop and animal production, soil science, and modern farming practices.

    Arts & Commercial Subjects

    • Government — how nations are governed: political systems, institutions, and the rule of law.
    • Economics — how individuals, businesses, and nations manage resources, trade, and money.
    • Literature in English — novels, drama, and poetry, building analytical and creative reading skills.
    • CRS / IRS — moral and religious education rooted in Christian or Islamic teachings.
    • Geography — landforms, climate, population, and how people relate to their environment.
    • Commerce — the basics of trade, business operations, and how markets work.
